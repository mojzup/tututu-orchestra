Tututu Orchestra

An organic mass of sounds harboring theatrical expressionism

Tututu Orchestra is a conducted improvisation orchestra of about 15 international musicians. Guided by the gestures of the conductress and by compositional fragments, they shape every performance live, and sometimes the audience joins in.
