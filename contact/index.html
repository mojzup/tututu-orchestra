About the orchestra

Tututu Orchestra is an international conducted improvisation orchestra of about 15 musicians. Compositional fragments drive guided improvisation, and the interplay between the orchestra and conductress Mojca Zupančič is at the centre of every performance, drawing on jazz, impressionism and folk music from around the world.

At the conductress's invitation the audience can join the sound-making, which blurs the line between performers and listeners.

We have performed in six countries, most recently at Notte Bianca in Valletta, Malta.
